Move your cursor, or touch and drag.

Interactions

CursorHead turns toward the pointer (8 directions, dead zone + hysteresis).
Touch & dragPut a finger on Nova and drag: the head follows it. (Elsewhere the page scrolls normally.)
TapBlink, then heart / sparkle / delighted in turn. Squash + a tiny haptic buzz.
Press & holdNova gets bashful while you hold (0.5s), softer buzz.
4 quick tapsDizzy.
Scroll the pageNova glances the way the page moves.
Leave it aloneAfter ~18s Nova dozes off (zzz). Any touch wakes it with a start.
